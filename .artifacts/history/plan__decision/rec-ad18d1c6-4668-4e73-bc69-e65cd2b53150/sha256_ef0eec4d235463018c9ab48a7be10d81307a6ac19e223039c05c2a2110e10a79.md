## Rationale

The dashboard has a done-in-24h role. Its membership changes with time alone, and the store-state digest does not see that. Re-rendering each interval and redrawing when the text differs catches both record writes and time-window changes. Rendering one store takes milliseconds.

## Counter-argument

Re-rendering on every tick costs more than a digest check on very large stores. A digest pre-check plus a coarse time tick would scale further.
