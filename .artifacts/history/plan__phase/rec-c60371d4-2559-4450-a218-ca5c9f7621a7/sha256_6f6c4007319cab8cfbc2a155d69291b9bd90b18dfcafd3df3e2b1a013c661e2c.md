## Problem

Claims shrink across handoffs: acceptance criterion prose and executor briefs re-summarize the work-item, and compound requirement ids let one acceptance cover a bundle. Deliver and repair work often has no requirement ids at all.

## Approach

not yet scoped — discuss when this phase is current. Direction: atomic requirement ids for deliver/repair, thin acceptances (no criterion prose), original-text briefs. evidence_kind is deferred to a later phase.

## Exit criteria

Decide at plan-phase. At minimum: plan-phase and skills require atomic requirement ids on deliver/repair work-items and acceptances without criterion prose, verified by skill/contract consistency tests.
