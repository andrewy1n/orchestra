## Problem
Session summaries do not say which se-workflow work a session did. Records carry no session id. They carry recorded_at (UTC), subject, payload.effort and record type. Transcripts carry cwd, start and end.

## Approach
Join by project and time window. A record belongs to a session when the record's store name matches the basename of the session's cwd and recorded_at falls inside the session's start to end window. A record inside two overlapping windows attaches to both and is marked ambiguous. A record in no window stays unattached. Read records through the adaptive-artifacts CLI with --read-only and keep only metadata, never record bodies. Extend scripts/session_report.py with a --records flag that adds per-session record counts by type and the efforts and tasks touched. Output without the flag stays unchanged.

## Exit criteria
A fixture with two sessions and five records attaches each record to the right session, marks the overlap record ambiguous on both, and leaves the outside and other-project records unattached. The real run with --records over this machine's store prints record counts for the sessions that overlap records and exits 0.
