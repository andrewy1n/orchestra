## Problem

Phase detail is one long scroll of nine panels, and it never shows the specification or design text: requirement wording, acceptance criteria, invariants, architecture, risks. Decisions show only their one-line choice, without rationale, alternatives, or what they superseded, and only for one phase. Long text is hard to read in a 40%-wide side pane, and the dashboard offers no way to read it elsewhere.

## Approach

Decisions: phase-screen-tabs, spec-full-text, decision-log-scope, pager-temp-file, simple-phase-spec-tab. Run as structured work: specification `spec-view`, design `spec-view`.

Phase detail becomes one tabbed phase screen: Overview, Spec, Design, Decisions, Evidence, Tasks. `p`, `s`, and `e` open it at Overview, Spec, or Evidence for the selected phase, or for the cursor row's phase on All phases. Spec and Design show the full record text as Markdown, and requirement lines open the requirement detail. The Decisions tab shows the phase's decisions with their rationale and counter-argument, toggles to the whole effort grouped by phase, and toggles superseded decisions linked from their successors. `o` writes the current tab's text to a temp file and opens it in `glow` or `$PAGER`. A phase with no specification shows its phase body and constraints on the Spec tab and hides Design and Evidence. The Evidence tab holds today's requirement list and integration result until `evidence-matrix` replaces it.

This phase edits the dashboard and does not change `contract/project-design.json` or `~/adaptive-artifacts`. It starts after `phase-selector` and `human-gates` are on main: it uses the selection and moves the landing and next-step lines onto Overview.

## Exit criteria

- Tab sets for structured and simple phases, decision log scopes, and superseded links hold against seeded stores (`tests/test_dashboard_model.py`, `tests/test_dashboard_phase_screen.py`).
- Keys, requirement links, Esc returning to the same tab, and `o` with the pager stubbed behave as specified in a Textual pilot (`tests/test_dashboard_phase_screen.py`).
- The full suite passes.
