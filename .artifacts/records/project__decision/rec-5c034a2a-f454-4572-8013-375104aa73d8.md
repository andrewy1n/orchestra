## Rationale

Evidence escalation alone never left `implementation` in dogfood, and "evidence increased" is judged by the agent being graded. A hard budget (default 2 re-dispatches or 2× estimate_minutes cumulative time, and 3 fix tasks per phase) is a backstop that forces Needs you regardless of whether the agent claims new evidence. Keeping the ladder for routing preserves diagnosis quality; the budget only decides when to stop.

## Counter-argument

A count of three failed assessments (as in closed draft PR #2) is simpler to test and explain, and would not need time tracking or phase fix-task counts. The cost is conflating routing with stopping, and losing the time-based backstop when a single re-dispatch burns the estimate.
