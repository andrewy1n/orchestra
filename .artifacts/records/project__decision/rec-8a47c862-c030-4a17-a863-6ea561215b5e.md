## Rationale

The user's pain is finished-but-unconvinced, not missing stop points. Requiring the packet before phase-done language fixes packaging. Mandating signed_by on every phase would slow non-product work without making Look clearer. Optional manual for demo kinds stays a follow-up.

## Counter-argument

Always requiring a human signature would guarantee eyes on every phase. That over-taxes attention and conflates "packet assembled" with "human approved."
