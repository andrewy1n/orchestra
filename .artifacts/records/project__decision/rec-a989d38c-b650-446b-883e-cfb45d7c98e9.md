## Rationale

`close_batch.py` today is a pure request→NDJSON transform with no store I/O. Carrying `red_check_run_id` (and the copied fail fields needed for the gate) keeps that shape: the caller that already resolved the green `check_run_id` also passes the red. User accepted this recommendation ("your call" on the recommended pair).

## Counter-argument

Store lookup by `criterion_id` would spare verify-work from assembling red ids and would catch a red that exists in the store but was omitted from the request. That couples close to adaptive-artifacts reads and makes offline/unit tests heavier.
