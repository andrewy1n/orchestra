## Rationale

References feel opaque because they appear as bare strings in lists and prose. Making known shapes clickable wherever they already show up teaches the store graph without a separate "map" UI.

## Counter-argument

Restricting links to dedicated Links chips would be safer and easier to test. That was rejected because most confusion happens inline in bodies and meta lines, not in a Links section the user may never open.
