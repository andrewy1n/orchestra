## Rationale

The page can clear the same human gates and backlog items the terminal can. Needs-you approve, reject, sign, and accept go through `dashboard.dismiss.dismiss_item`, including its displayed-revision check. Backlog dismiss and promote go through `dashboard.backlog`. The server binds `127.0.0.1` and takes the signer from `dashboard.dismiss.default_signer`, so the browser cannot choose who signed. Any local process can call that port; that is the same trust boundary as a terminal on this machine.
