## Rationale

Assessments can already say `next: plan`, but nothing consumes that signal. Teaching plan-phase to add the missing task(s) and re-open plan review for the delta closes the loop without inventing a new skill, and matches how discuss already consumes `specify` and `design`.

## Counter-argument

Deferring `next: plan` would shrink this phase to execute/verify only. The cost is leaving a documented dead-end in the assessment ladder until a later phase, while agents keep producing `next: plan` with no handler.
