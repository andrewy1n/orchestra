## Rationale

Free-text `result` and `method` already let malformed values into the store (`result: tdd`, garbage methods). Declaring enums in the contract (via existing `payload_enum`) makes illegal creates fail validation. That overturns the evidence-loop guidance that `result` must not be a contract enum, which optimized for openness over machine checks.

## Counter-argument

Keeping `result` free-form avoids churn in evidence-loop tests and verify-work wording, and leaves room for new result labels without a contract edit. Soft validation in the runner alone would catch new writes but not raw `adaptive-artifacts create`.
