## Rationale

The home side rail is for actionable interruptions. A full journal list competes with Needs you and Backlog and is what made the screen feel overwhelming. A one-line open-count link keeps discoverability without dumping entries.

## Counter-argument

Keeping a short preview of open journal items (≤3) would show substance without a navigation hop. That was rejected because even a short list reintroduces scanning noise on the home screen the calm-home milestone is trying to remove.
