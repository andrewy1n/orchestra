## Rationale

A dedicated hash route keeps journal browsing addressable and matches the spike's "All journal" page. Reusing GET /api/journal avoids new endpoints. Open-vs-earlier plus search mirrors how people skim a long journal.

## Counter-argument

A modal overlay from the home rail would avoid a new route. That was rejected because long journals need filterable scrolling space of their own, not a dialog stacked on the home layout.
