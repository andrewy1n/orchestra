## Rationale

The brief's ordered stack matches the failure modes: forged close first, then claim shrinkage, then oracle separation, then evidence level, then code-side gap reports. Shipping the full sequence as sequential phases keeps each phase reviewable and avoids a vertical slice that parks half the differentiator indefinitely.

## Counter-argument

A vertical slice (foundation + one oracle loop) would prove the witness claim sooner and might reveal that later phases need reshaping. The cost is delaying atomic-claim and evidence-kind work that already have clear designs in the brief.
