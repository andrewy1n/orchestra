## Rationale

#7 is the dependency root for fix-task routing that #9 and #10 need. Shipping four phases in that order keeps each Landing reviewable and matches the umbrella issue's suggested sequence. #8 is independent and last so human-gate UX does not block the verification-loop payoff. #11 stays a tracking backlog item, not a phase.

## Counter-argument

Merging #7+#10+#9 into one phase would land the full verification loop faster in calendar time and avoid three Landing cycles. The cost is a large phase that is hard to plan-review and that delays any usable retry path until reviewer and tiered verification also ship.
