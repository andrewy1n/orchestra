## Rationale

Manual checks and plan review are scarce independent human gates. One-keypress dismiss without evidence, rationale, or revision guarding makes those gates meaningless and can approve a plan the user never saw after an agent update. Full #8 (detail, confirm, notes, displayed revision, reject, constraint cleanup) closes that hole in one phase.

## Counter-argument

Confirm + revision only would shrink UI work and skip new payload fields. The cost is weak reject/rationale semantics and harder auditing of why a gate cleared.
