## Rationale

The phase approach is the product choice. The executor still needs a recipe, and today that recipe is settled in conversation at execution time. Writing it on the task makes plan review the last moment to correct it.

## Counter-argument

A recipe on the phase alone is less to write, and many tasks only restate it. Per-task recipes make the plan longer and can drift from the phase approach.
