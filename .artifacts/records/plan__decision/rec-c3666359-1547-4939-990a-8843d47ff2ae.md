## Rationale

Structured work must still settle approach before tasks exist. Promoting a backlog item therefore closes or supersedes the item and hands off to discuss with a new phase for that effort (or to an incidental work-item when the kind fits). That reuses the existing loop instead of inventing a side door that materializes tasks inside an in-progress phase.

## Counter-argument

Auto-materializing work-items on promote would clear the backlog faster and skip a discuss round-trip. The cost is bypassing specification and design for work that was parked precisely because it was not ready to plan.
