## Rationale

Stale Needs-you items are usually forgotten closes, not unresolved blockers. Reminding on engage surfaces them without stopping unrelated work on the same focus (for example continuing a phase while an old finding still needs a separate decision).

## Counter-argument

Gating engage until Needs you is cleared would force cleanup and stop the panel from accumulating forever; the cost is blocking legitimate progress when an old finding or unsigned check is unrelated to the current ask.
