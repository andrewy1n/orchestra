## Rationale

Affidavit already exposes observation → bind → recheck and verdict/attest. The forgeable `runner: "aff"` stamp was a parallel fake evidence plane. Stamping Orchestra runs honestly and gating manifest closes on Affidavit CLI verbs fixes trust without inventing `aff run` or extending attest to check-runs. The user chose this over attest-seal and skills-only.

## Counter-argument

Sealing check-runs with `aff attest` (approach B) would make operational runs themselves unforgeable under a key allowlist. That strengthens the Orchestra plane but grows Affidavit into workflow check-run shape and duplicates the claim path Affidavit already has.
