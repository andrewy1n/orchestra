## Rationale

The plugin must not invent a multi-hour gate for every consumer repo. Putting `phase_gate` on `project-design.json` (alongside existing `assessment`) makes suite mode, command, and hardness versioned with the project, readable by skills and tests, and changeable only by an explicit contract edit—not mid-Landing improvisation.

## Counter-argument

Keeping the hardcoded full suite and only softening this repo (smoke or advisory docs) would relieve local pain faster with less surface area. That leaves every other install stuck with the same universal rule and keeps "tier-2" as opaque jargon.
