## Rationale

Requirement ids are unique only within a phase, so the phase is the natural home. It adds no key or tab, keeps the main pane about what is happening now, and fits 60 columns.

## Counter-argument

A separate screen shows unverified requirements across every phase at once, which matters once several structured phases are open together.
