## Rationale

#9 and #10 both route failures through fix tasks under a retry budget. Shipping re-dispatch, fix tasks, budget, `next: plan`, and attempt display together gives those phases a shared primitive and removes the closing-verification stall in one Landing. Partial ships would leave verification failures stuck after siblings are `done`.

## Counter-argument

Core-only (re-dispatch + budget, defer fix tasks) would land a smaller first PR and prove the stall fix for ordinary task failures sooner. The cost is a second phase before #9/#10 can safely create fix tasks, and closing-verification failures keep stalling until that follow-on.
