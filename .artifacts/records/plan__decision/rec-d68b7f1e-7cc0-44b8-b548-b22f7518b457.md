## Rationale

The sitting line and the executor are already stored, and title suffixes are how a 60-column pane can show them. That display waits until `size` and `estimate_minutes` are fields the contract accepts. A screen built on the sitting line would present session shape as if it were a duration.

## Counter-argument

Both the sitting line and the executor are already on the records, so the table could show them in this phase. Waiting couples a small display change to a contract change it does not need.
