## Problem

An effort has one `current-position`. Two chats on two phases of one effort overwrite each other, which pushes agents to split one product area into several efforts. Handoff also groups by `subject`, so task-scoped records render as if they were efforts.

## Approach

- A running phase gets its own `current-position`: `subject` = phase slug, `payload.effort` = focus, `payload.phase` = phase slug, `scope` = `phase`. The current-claim identity check then allows one active position per phase.
- The effort-level position (`subject` = focus) stays as the summary between phases; only phase close supersedes it.
- plan-phase creates the phase position when it promotes the phase. execute-phase closes it (`status: closed`) at phase close and supersedes the effort position.
- With several phases `in_progress`, execute-phase and plan-phase use the phase named in context (engage or the user), and ask when it is unclear.
- Handoff groups by `effort` (falls back to `subject`), so phase positions and task records render under their effort.

## Exit criteria

- Two phase positions for one effort coexist, validate, and render under the effort section of handoff; closing one hides it.
- A work-item renders under its effort in handoff, not under its task slug.
- plan-phase, execute-phase and kinds-and-focus document the per-phase position; skill/contract consistency tests pass.
