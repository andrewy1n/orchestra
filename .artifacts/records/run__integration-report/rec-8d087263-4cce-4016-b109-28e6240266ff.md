## Interfaces

`default_phase`, `resolve_selection`, `pick`, and `step_selection` in `dashboard/selection.py` feed `EffortPane.show` and `render_once`. `scoped` is what `tasks.tab_tasks`, `tasks.counts`, and `phase_progress` receive as the selected phase. `selector_text` and `wave_strip` render on the same selected subject. `merge_activity` is what `activity_lines` prints, and that call uses the unscoped effort view. `visible_efforts` is what the tab bar uses. `task_columns` no longer includes wave.

## Assumptions

Most recent activity is the latest `recorded_at` on activity items for a phase's tasks. `?` pushes a help screen of the active bindings, which is the fallback the design allowed when a key panel was not used. A phase with no activity ranks by ordinal.

## Invariants

Selection stays out of `load_snapshot`. Needs you and Activity read the unscoped view. All phases still uses grouped section rows. An effort with no phases gets no selector and no wave strip. The status-line segment is unchanged.
