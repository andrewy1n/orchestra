## Problem

Claims shrink across handoffs: acceptance criterion prose and executor briefs re-summarize the work-item, and compound requirement ids let one acceptance cover a bundle. Deliver and repair work often has no requirement ids at all.

## Approach

Skills + contract claim chain (decisions: `atomic-claims-enforcement`, `thin-acceptance-shape`, `atomic-deliver-repair-ids`, `original-text-briefs`). Spec/design: `atomic-claims`.

1. Contract: move `criterion` to optional_payload; drop it from brief/plan/verification acceptance `requires_payload`.
2. Skills: plan-phase + discuss require atomic deliver/repair requirement ids and thin acceptances; execute-phase briefs quote original work-item and requirement text by id.
3. Tests: rewrite plan-trace / consistency expectations for the new rules.

No close-batch lint, oracle, or evidence_kind in this phase.

**Session shape:** one sitting
**Executor:** subagent
**Human stops:** plan review; Landing merge into `main` (no push)
**Collision notes:** owns `contract/project-design.json`, `skills/plan-phase`, `skills/discuss`, `skills/execute-phase` (and verify-work wording if needed), `tests/test_plan_trace.py`. Waves are sequential (contract → skills → tests); do not parallelize a wave across those shared files.

## Exit criteria

- plan-phase, discuss, and execute-phase require atomic deliver/repair requirement ids, thin acceptances, and original-text briefs; examples match the contract.
- `criterion` is optional on acceptance; brief/plan/verification acceptance roles do not require it.
- `python3 -m pytest tests/test_skill_contract_consistency.py tests/test_plan_trace.py -q` is green.
- Integration: a planned deliver work-item + thin acceptance shape validates through the real adaptive-artifacts create/capture path used by plan-phase (covered by plan-trace / consistency tests that drive the CLI).
