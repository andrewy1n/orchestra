## Description

R1: aff verdict fails a claim when a declared support file hash differs between base and head without a covering signed amendment.
R2: aff verdict fails a claim when the manifest runner argv digest differs between base and head without a covering signed amendment.

## Approach

1. Add Manifest.support_files (tuple of relpaths; absent means empty). Claims may list support_files too; the frozen set for a claim is the union of claim and manifest lists.
2. Add runner_digest(runner) -> sha256 of canonical argv lines (one element per line).
3. Extend amendment canonical_payload and authorize so support hashes and runner digests chain like oracle_hashes (old/new support_hashes and old/new runner_digest fields; omit means empty/unchanged as needed for back-compat with existing amendments that only touch tests/hashes).
4. In verdict, hash support paths at base and head; compare runner digests; require authorize to cover support and runner deltas the same way as oracle hash deltas. Fail the claim when they drift without a covering chain.
5. Keep existing oracle-hash and amendment behaviors. Do not edit cli.py (witness-lint owns lint CLI).
6. Tests in test_manifest/test_amendment/test_verdict for support-only drift, runner-only drift, and a signed amendment that authorizes a support or runner change.
