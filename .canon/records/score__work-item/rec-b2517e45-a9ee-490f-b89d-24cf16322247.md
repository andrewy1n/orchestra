## Description

R2: `orchestra place <effort> <area>` creates an active `composer:placement` whose subject is the effort slug.
R3: `orchestra place` on an effort that already has a placement supersedes that placement.
R4: `orchestra place` exits nonzero when the effort does not exist.
R5: `orchestra place` exits nonzero when the area does not exist.
R9: `canon validate` passes on a store that holds placements.
R11: After `orchestra place` on an already placed effort, exactly one placement for that effort is active.
R12: `orchestra place` writes no record when the effort does not exist.
R13: `orchestra place` writes no record when the area does not exist.

## Approach

Add `orchestra/composer/placement.py` with `PlacementError` and `place(project, effort, area)`. It resolves the effort as `score:effort` and the area as `composer:area`, and raises `PlacementError` when either is missing. It creates the placement with subject = effort slug and payload `{"area": ...}`, or supersedes the active one with its current revision. Add the `place` subcommand in `orchestra/cli.py` next to `idea`, with `_cmd_place` written like `_cmd_idea`: print the record on success, print the error to stderr and return 1 on `PlacementError`. Update `orchestra/composer/AGENTS.md` to list placement.
