## Description

Orchestra skills still tell an agent to create a `tdd` or `check` check-run and a `project:oracle-amendment`. Gate answers have to come from `aff`.

## Approach

1. Add `~/orchestra/tests/test_skill_evidence_boundary.py`. It scans `skills/**/*.md` and fails when a file contains a `create` or `correct` of `project:check-run` whose method is `tdd` or `check`, or a `create` or `supersede` of `project:oracle-amendment`. A fixture skill with those writes fails the scanner. The migrated skills pass. A `manual` or `review` check-run, and the suite module's own check-run write, stay allowed. A second test, selected with `-k verdict`, asserts `execute-phase` and `verify-work` run `aff verdict` for a claim in `.affidavit/manifest.json` and treat a non-zero exit as not done.
2. Update `skills/execute-phase/SKILL.md` and `skills/verify-work/SKILL.md` so a `tdd` or `check` result comes from `aff check`, not from creating the check-run. Remove the `project:oracle-amendment` create and supersede snippets. Point an oracle hash or test-id change at a signed amendment in `.affidavit/manifest.json`, accepted or rejected by `aff verdict`.
3. Do not edit `skills/ensure-store.md`. Do not change the `git commit -m "<task-slug>"` lines; `conventional-commits` owns those. Do not remove `manual`, `review`, or suite check-run writes.
