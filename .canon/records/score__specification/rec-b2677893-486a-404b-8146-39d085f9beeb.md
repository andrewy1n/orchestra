## Requirements

R1: `aff claim add` writes a new claim entry into the committed manifest format without requiring orchestra.

R2: `aff freeze` records oracle (and support) file hashes for claims into the manifest.

R3: A lint reports a compound claim (more than one observable behavior in the claim text, per the phase's lint rules).

R4: `aff verdict` accepts a claim witnessed by a valid allowlist signature (human-signed claim) when the signature verifies.

R5: `aff verdict` rejects a human-signed claim witness that is missing or does not verify.

## Acceptance criteria

- A clean repo can produce a valid manifest via claim add + freeze and get a verdict without orchestra or adaptive-artifacts on PATH.
- Lint flags a fixture compound claim.
- Signed human claim passes; unsigned/invalid fails.

## Constraints

- CLI remains free of store tokens in `affidavit/`.
- Signing reuses allowlist/SSH patterns from amendments where practical.
- Does not remove the need for red-green oracles on automated claims.

## Non-goals

- Full TUI review product.
- Orchestra-only authoring paths as the primary interface.

## Invariants

- Human-signed acceptance requires verify success.
- Manifest remains the authority.

## Assumptions

- Freeze covers support files once frozen-environment exists.
