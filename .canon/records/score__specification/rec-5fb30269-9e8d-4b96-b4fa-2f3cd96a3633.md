## Requirements

R1: The project contract defines a `project:evidence-binding` record type whose payload names an observation id, a claim id, and pin revisions (base and head).
R2: `aff bind` creates an evidence-binding that attaches an existing evidence-observation to a claim under those pins.
R3: `aff unbind` retires an evidence-binding so that observation no longer earns that claim.
R4: `aff recheck` re-evaluates a bound claim under its pins and exits non-zero when the binding no longer holds.
R5: After `aff verdict` finishes judging with a usable store, it creates an evidence-observation and does not create an evidence-binding.
R6: Unbound evidence-observations remain listable via `aff trail`.
R7: A pytest accepts only when a fixture shows: record an unbound observation, `aff bind` it to a claim, then `aff recheck` for that claim exits 0 while the pins still hold.

## Acceptance criteria

- Contract change validates under adaptive-artifacts.
- `aff bind` / `unbind` / `recheck` are CLI verbs with tests covering create, retire, and failed recheck.
- Verdict with a store still records an observation and creates zero bindings.
- `aff trail` lists unbound observations after a verdict or audit-style record.
- Phase pytest for the touched files passes.

## Constraints

- Do not import adaptive-artifacts Python packages from `affidavit/`.
- Claims and `oracle_hashes` remain in `.affidavit/manifest.json` only.
- Observation records stay append-only; earning a claim is not done by patching `claim_ids` on the observation.
- Orchestra skill edits are out of scope (phase `evidence-workflow`).
- Audit/probe ingestion is out of scope (phase `evidence-technique-ingest`).

## Non-goals

- Auto-binding any technique on record.
- Public substrate verbs (stimulate/intervene).
- Changing attestation format.
- Encoding record-vs-bind policy as aff modes.

## Invariants

- Recording an observation is not acceptance of a claim.
- Store writes use the public CLI only.
- Judge logic for verdict (red/green, hashes, amendments) is unchanged in meaning; bind/recheck are additive.

## Assumptions

- `evidence-interface` observation type and `aff trail` already ship.
- Subject repos that bind have a usable adaptive-artifacts store.
