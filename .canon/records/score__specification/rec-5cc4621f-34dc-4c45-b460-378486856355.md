## Requirements

R1: `aff audit` runs declared probes (a command or function plus inputs) at base and at head and compares results.

R2: The audit reports a difference that no cited claim id accounts for.

R3: The audit does not report a difference that is covered by a cited claim id.

R4: On the `red-first` range from the differential spike, the audit reports the `revision`-on-`tdd` relaxation as a difference no claim covers.

## Acceptance criteria

- Probe declaration schema lives with the manifest or an audit config committed in-repo.
- Default comparison includes a coarse CLI-level mode.
- R2–R4 are test-covered with fixtures derived from the spike.

## Constraints

- Extends the audit surface from phase-end-evidence; keep one `aff audit` entry point.
- Program flags diffs; only a cited claim id or a human settles them.
- Phase-end reviewer runs on this phase (campaign milestone).

## Non-goals

- Auto-discovering all pure functions.
- Replacing verdict.

## Invariants

- Claim-covered diffs stay quiet.
- Unclaimed diffs are visible in the report.

## Assumptions

- Spike `red-first` range remains reproducible or is vendored as a fixture.
