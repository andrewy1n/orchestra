## Rationale

The dashboard backlog panel keeps today's behavior: it lists open `composer:idea` records that carry an effort, grouped by effort. Only the type changes. Ideas without an effort appear on the roadmap view in phase `idea-triage`. This keeps the dashboard change small and avoids building two views of the same list.

## Counter-argument

An Ideas panel for effortless ideas would show new ideas as soon as `orchestra idea` exists, grouped by area. Until phase `idea-triage` lands, ideas captured without an effort are visible nowhere on the dashboard.
