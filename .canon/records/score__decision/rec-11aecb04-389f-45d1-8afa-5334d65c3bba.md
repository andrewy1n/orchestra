## Rationale

The six optimization areas and `bin/aa` were selected together from one trace synthesis. Splitting would delay the highest-friction wins (revision default, short names) behind a second Landing while still requiring the same compatibility discipline. One phase keeps plan-phase and verify coherent against a single exit set.

## Counter-argument

A core-then-polish split (option B) would land agent-facing wins sooner with less review surface, and would isolate the views/ immutability exemption—the riskiest validate change—in a follow-up phase if it regresses hook-stop.
