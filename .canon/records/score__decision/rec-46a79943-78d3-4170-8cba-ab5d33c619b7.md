## Rationale

Half of today's 20,665-character handoff is record-id links, and Latest Proof and Backlog are another 8,800. With only the needed roles and no links the efforts take about 6,900 characters. A budget guard is still needed because the handoff grows with every effort and finding. Dropping whole efforts, oldest position first, keeps what the agent does see complete and keeps active work.

## Counter-argument

An index-only hook stays small however many efforts exist and never hides an effort's detail behind a budget. The compact view can still drop efforts silently from the agent's attention when the store grows, and it duplicates role selection that `orchestra handoff` already owns.
