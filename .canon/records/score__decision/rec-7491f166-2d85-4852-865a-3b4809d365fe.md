## Rationale

The specification and the design are the two authored documents that plan-phase reads before it writes tasks. An unknown in either becomes a guess in code. The phase body is excluded because a placeholder there honestly says "decide at plan-phase".

## Counter-argument

Spec Kit applies the marker to the specification only. Specification text is about what and why, where an open question is a real gap. A design unknown is a decision, and settle-phase already records decisions with the user. A marker rule on the design duplicates that step and adds a second place to check for the same unknown.
