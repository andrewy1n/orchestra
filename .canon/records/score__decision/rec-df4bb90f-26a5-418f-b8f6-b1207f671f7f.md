## Rationale

`canon` sections are all-or-nothing per type, and its migrations cannot backfill bodies. Of 17 active efforts, 16 have an empty body. Sections on `score:effort` would break all 16 unless someone invents briefs for them. A separate `score:brief` type, keyed by the effort slug, validates the four sections only where a brief exists. It follows how `specification` and `design` already sit beside `phase`.

## Counter-argument

One record that holds the goal and the brief is the simpler model. One read returns both, and the brief cannot drift from the goal or be forgotten. The cost today is 16 invented bodies for old efforts. A future `canon` migration step that backfills bodies would remove that cost, and then the separate type is an extra read and an extra write for no gain.
