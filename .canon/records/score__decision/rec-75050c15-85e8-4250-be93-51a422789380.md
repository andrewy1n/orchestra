## Rationale

The side pane is about 80 columns wide. A split beside the task table leaves neither side readable there. A pushed screen uses the full pane and keeps the dashboard state underneath.

## Counter-argument

A split lets you scan tasks and details together on a wide popup, and a pushed screen hides the dashboard while it is open.
