## Rationale

A rail that lists the siblings of the current level always answers "what else is here". The product index needs none, because its cards are the list. The effort rail gets shorter, because it holds only the efforts of one area.

## Counter-argument

A flat rail of every effort is the current, known behavior. It reaches any effort in one click from any page, while the sibling rail needs a trip through the area to change areas. The show-finished toggle and the dots already work there.
