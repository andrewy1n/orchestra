## Rationale

The dashboard effort deleted the stdlib server and moved the app to dashboard/backend/server. A shared route table existed to keep two servers in step. With one FastAPI app, its routes are the list, and the endpoint-shape fixtures and the committed OpenAPI schema pin them.

## Counter-argument

A table still documents the routes in one place. The OpenAPI schema and the per-route fixtures already do that, and a second list would drift.
