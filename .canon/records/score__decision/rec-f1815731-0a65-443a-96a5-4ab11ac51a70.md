## Rationale

Re-opening a `done` sibling would erase a closed acceptance trail and fight the staged lifecycle. Creating a new fix work-item with its own acceptance, and adding a `depends_on` from the verification task to it, reuses `derived.ready` so verification re-dispatches automatically once the fix lands. The same shape serves tiered verification and the phase-end reviewer later.

## Counter-argument

Re-opening the guilty `done` task would avoid proliferating fix slugs and keep blame on the original work-item. The cost is rewriting history on a closed lifecycle state and confusing dashboards that treat `done` as terminal for that subject.
