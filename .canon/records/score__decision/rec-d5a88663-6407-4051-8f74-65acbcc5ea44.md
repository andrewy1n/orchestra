## Rationale

Defaulting an absent kind to unit keeps existing manifests valid while any declared interaction claim is held to the template allowlist. Printing the argv through the CLI lets fixtures and users copy the shipped template into the manifest runner without importing package modules.

## Counter-argument

Rejecting a missing kind would force every claim to say what it is. It also invalidates every committed manifest, which this phase does not migrate.
