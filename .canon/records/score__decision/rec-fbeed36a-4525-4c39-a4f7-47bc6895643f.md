## Rationale

Grandfathering `aff` keeps the forgeable stamp meaningful at close. A clean refuse forces re-run under `orchestra` and removes dual-marker ambiguity. The user chose refuse over grandfather.

## Counter-argument

Grandfathering `aff` or `orchestra` during a transition would avoid breaking in-flight closes and historical dogfood closes that are re-verified. The cost is prolonged ambiguity about what the stamp means.
