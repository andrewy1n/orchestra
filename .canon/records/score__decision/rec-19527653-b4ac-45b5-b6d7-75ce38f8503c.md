## Rationale

Issue #6 specifies git descendant-or-equal for SHA revisions, with dirty red allowed only when `diff_sha256` is recorded. That ties the green evidence to the same line of work as the red and aligns with dirty-pass policy (fails may be dirty; digests identify the tree). User accepted this recommendation.

## Counter-argument

Ordering only by `recorded_at` (plus requiring a dirty digest) is simpler and needs no git. It is weaker against replay or reordered evidence and does not prove the green commit descends from the red's tree.
