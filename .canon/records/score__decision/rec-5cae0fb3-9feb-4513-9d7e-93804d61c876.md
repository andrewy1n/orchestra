## Rationale

The status tabs are unchanged. The stepper already looked like a phase picker, and users tried to use it as one; making it the selector removes that mismatch. The done count now opens the picker instead of expanding titles.

## Counter-argument

A display-only stepper cannot be misread as interactive state, and the selection could live on its own line instead.
