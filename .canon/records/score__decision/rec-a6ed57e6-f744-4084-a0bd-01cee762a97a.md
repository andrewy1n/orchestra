## Rationale

A phase is a level of the drill-down, like the product, the area and the effort. A page in the main pane has the breadcrumb, the left rail and a stable URL, as the other levels have. A modal hides the breadcrumb and makes the phase look like a detail of the effort.

## Counter-argument

A full-screen dialog keeps the working view models, focus handling and Esc behavior, and changes only the size. It is a much smaller change with less risk to the overlay tests. A page rewrite touches the largest state module in the frontend.
