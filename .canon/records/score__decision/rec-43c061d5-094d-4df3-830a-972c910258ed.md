## Rationale

Biome is one dependency and one config file, and it lints and formats. On the current UI source with full Svelte support on, Biome 2.6.4 reports 1 false `noUnusedVariables` finding. `svelte-check` already covers template type errors and accessibility.

## Counter-argument

ESLint with eslint-plugin-svelte and typescript-eslint has Svelte-specific rules and type-aware TypeScript rules that Biome lacks. Biome's Svelte support is experimental and its option can change in a minor release. Pinning the exact version limits the exposure but does not remove it.
