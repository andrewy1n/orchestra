## Rationale

Product planning sits above work planning: an idea targets or becomes an effort. `composer:idea` carries an optional `effort` reference to `score:effort`, and score does not change. composer imports only canon and score.

## Counter-argument

With composer below score, composer works in a store without score, and an effort can cite the idea it came from. That keeps provenance on the effort, where execution readers look, instead of on the idea.
