## Rationale

Auditability for humans and future agents needs a queryable trail that survives sessions. The adaptive-artifacts store already provides typed records, revisions, and list/get. Putting observations there is the long-term fit for an evidence interface, while claims and frozen witnesses stay in the committed `.affidavit/manifest.json` (unchanged authority for what is asserted).

## Counter-argument

A git-local `.affidavit/observations/` ledger keeps standalone `aff` fully store-free and makes replay bit-identical to the tree. Store-backed observations couple technique runs to store presence and revise the earlier "aff does not read a record store" seam. That cost is accepted for queryability and multi-agent handoff; the seam is narrowed via CLI, not runtime import.
