## Rationale

Choosing the area of an effort is a product judgment. For example, `workflow-loop` and `evidence-layer` may need areas that do not exist yet. A wrong mapping would mislead the area pages. The agent proposes a mapping and the user confirms it before the backfill task runs.

## Counter-argument

Leaving every effort unassigned removes a human gate from the phase. The area pages already show an Unassigned group, and the user can place efforts later with the CLI. The agent would not propose a mapping that the user then has to review.
