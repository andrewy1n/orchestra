## Rationale

The Textual dashboard already limits the Journal panel to repair and evaluate efforts. Matching that on the web avoids a journal affordance on deliver efforts where the panel was never part of the product vocabulary.

## Counter-argument

Showing Journal for every effort would be simpler and always discoverable. That was rejected to stay consistent with `kind-journal-panel` and to keep deliver home screens quieter.
