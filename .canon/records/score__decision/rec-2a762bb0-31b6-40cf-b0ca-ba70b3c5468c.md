## Rationale

The runtime has no third-party dependencies today. Plain ANSI (clear, home, bold headings) covers a read-only redraw loop. NO_COLOR and a non-tty stdout turn colour off.

## Counter-argument

rich would give panels, colour and width handling for free, and a later interactive dashboard may need a TUI library anyway.
