## Rationale

The monorepo plan names `orchestra plan` and `orchestra run` as task verbs and never says what they do. The CLI already has `orchestra view plan`, `orchestra verify`, and `orchestra handoff`. The procedures are the `plan-phase` and `execute-phase` skills. `orchestra idea` belongs to the composer effort. Adding the two verbs would duplicate those surfaces.

## Counter-argument

An `orchestra plan` alias of `orchestra view plan` would make the monorepo verb exist without a new procedure. Callers who read the plan literally would find the word. It would also make two commands for one view, and it still leaves `orchestra run` undefined.
