## Evidence

`git ls-files uv.lock` prints nothing. `.git/info/exclude` line 8 is `uv.lock`. Every job in `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs `uv sync --locked --group dev`.

## Consequence

Every CI job fails at install on a fresh checkout. The settled constraint says CI installs with `--locked`, and `ci-after-wheel-contents` assumed a tracked lock.

## Follow-up

The user decides: track `uv.lock` (remove it from `.git/info/exclude` and commit the lock), or drop `--locked`.
