## Architecture

orchestra/views.py gains a hook-handoff renderer next to HANDOFF. It reuses the GOAL, POSITION, in-progress, blocking-question, needs-human and unsigned-manual-check roles, renders lines without the id link, and returns one section per live effort ordered by that effort's position recorded_at, newest first. hook_constraints returns active constraints for every live effort. orchestra/stage/hooks.py cmd_start assembles protocol, constraints, then effort sections until the next section would pass HOOK_CONTEXT_BUDGET (9,000), then one line naming the omitted efforts. MAX_HANDOFF_BYTES and _truncate go away.

## Interfaces

views.hook_handoff_sections(records) -> list[tuple[str, str]] (effort slug, section text). views.hook_constraints(records, limit) keeps its signature and returns constraints of all live efforts. hooks.HOOK_CONTEXT_BUDGET = 9000. MAX_HOOK_CONSTRAINTS rises to 20.

## Decisions

hook-context-shape, hook-constraints-scope.

## Invariants

The start hook context never exceeds HOOK_CONTEXT_BUDGET plus the omitted-efforts line and footer. `orchestra handoff` output is byte-identical to before.

## Assumptions

Each effort has at most one active effort-scoped position; an effort with none sorts last.

## Tradeoffs

The 1,000-character gap between budget and cap absorbs the omitted-efforts line and footer instead of measuring them exactly. Constraints are not budgeted separately; at 20 × ~250 characters they leave about 3,700 characters for efforts in the worst case.

## Risks

A single effort with many Needs Human findings can exceed the budget alone and be omitted entirely; the pointer line still names it.
