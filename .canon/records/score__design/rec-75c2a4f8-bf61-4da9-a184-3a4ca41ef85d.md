## Architecture

- `router.ts` gains the `effort` route (`#/effort/<name>`). `home` becomes the product index. `polling.selectedEffort` follows the route, so the URL is the source of truth.
- `lib/state/areas.ts` holds pure functions: `groupByArea(efforts, areas)`, `areaRollup(efforts, area)`, `railItems(route, snapshot, areas)` and `breadcrumbParts(route, snapshot, areas)`. They are unit tested without a DOM.
- New components: `Breadcrumb.svelte` (replaces the top-bar crumbs), `ProductIndex.svelte` with `AreaCard.svelte` (one anchor, no button), and `EffortRow.svelte` shared by the area page.
- `Area.svelte` is rewritten: scope, rollup stats, merged read-only Needs-you list, Efforts rows, Ideas section, "Started as efforts" lines.
- `EffortRail.svelte` becomes a `Rail.svelte` that renders by level: none on the product index, areas on the area page, same-area efforts on the effort page.
- `Home.svelte` gains the area chip beside the effort title. Its body is unchanged.
- Backend: `dashboard/backend/areas.py` reads `composer:placement` and adds a promoted idea to the area where its effort is placed. The response shape does not change.
- `detail.ts` gains `effortHref(name)`.

## Interfaces

- Routes: `#/` product index, `#/area/<subject>`, `#/effort/<name>`. The routes `#/journal/...`, `#/phase/...`, `#/task/...` and the others are unchanged.
- `GET /api/areas`: each `AreaView.ideas` entry may have `status: "promoted"` with `effort` set. No new fields.
- `Breadcrumb` takes the route, the snapshot and the areas, and renders anchors for every part but the last.

## Decisions

- `landing-product-index`: `#/` shows the product index.
- `rail-siblings`: the left rail lists the siblings of the current level.
- `area-needs-read-only`: Needs-you on the area page is a read-only list that links to the effort.
- `area-join-client-side`: group efforts in the browser. Add promoted ideas in the backend.

## Invariants

- Hash routing only. One served document.
- The product index and the area page write nothing.
- An idea never renders among the efforts.
- The URL decides the selected effort.

## Assumptions

- Phase `effort-area-link` is merged, so `area` is on every snapshot effort.
- The Home screen needs no change other than the chip.
- The Playwright seed store can hold an area and a placement.

## Tradeoffs

- Moving the effort Home to `#/effort/<name>` changes the landing page. Old bookmarks to `#/` now show the product index.
- The sibling rail needs a visit to the area page to change area. The breadcrumb makes that one click.
- The client-side grouping puts rollup rules in the frontend. Pure functions with unit tests keep them checkable.

## Risks

- Many existing tests assume that `#/` shows an effort. Mitigation: update them in one task, and keep R27 as an explicit requirement.
- The router change breaks a deep link. Mitigation: R27 and the existing deep-link tests.
- A missing placement leaves an effort in the "Not in an area" group on a store that is not backfilled. Mitigation: the group is part of the design.
- The Playwright seed has no area. Mitigation: extend `e2e/serve.py` to seed one.
