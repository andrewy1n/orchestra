## Architecture

Observations remain a trail of technique runs (`project:evidence-observation`). Earning is a separate current-claim: `project:evidence-binding` points at an observation id, a claim id, and pin revisions.

```
aff verdict --> observation (unbound)
aff bind    --> evidence-binding (observation + claim + pins)
aff unbind  --> retire binding
aff recheck --> re-evaluate bound claim under pins
aff trail   --> list observations (bound or not)
```

Claims stay in `.affidavit/manifest.json`. Store writes go through the `adaptive-artifacts` CLI subprocess only.

## Interfaces

- Contract: `project:evidence-binding` with payload at least `observation_id`, `claim_id`, `base_rev`, `head_rev` (names may match existing pin field conventions).
- CLI: `aff bind`, `aff unbind`, `aff recheck` under `--cwd` like other verbs.
- Observation `claim_ids` is non-authoritative context at most; earning is only via an active binding.

## Decisions

- `binding-record`
- `verdict-no-auto-bind`

## Invariants

- No technique auto-creates a binding on record in this phase.
- Unbound observations stay queryable.
- Bind does not rewrite observation payload to assert earn.

## Assumptions

- `evidence-interface` recorder and trail exist.
- Recheck can invoke existing judge/re-run paths under the binding’s pins.

## Tradeoffs

- Extra record type and verbs vs mutating observations: chose separate binding for a clear earn act.
- Always-unbound verdict vs auto-bind on pass: chose unbound to keep record ≠ earn.

## Risks

- Agents keep treating a green verdict observation as acceptance until `evidence-workflow` teaches bind — mitigate with skill phase next and clear CLI docs.
- Contract drift with orchestra until the binding type is mirrored when skills depend on it.
