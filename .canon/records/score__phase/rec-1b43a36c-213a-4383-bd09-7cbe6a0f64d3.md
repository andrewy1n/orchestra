## Problem

`engage` starts from a request in chat. It cannot start an effort from a triaged idea.

## Approach

Chosen: `init` and `settle-phase` write the promotion (`idea-promotion-writer`). `engage` takes an open `composer:idea` as input, sets focus from the idea's effort or routes to `init` for a new effort, and carries `idea: <subject>` in the routing line. After `init` creates the effort, or `settle-phase` creates the phase, that skill supersedes the idea to `promoted` with the effort and phase refs.

Session shape: one sitting. Waves: `engage-idea-input` and `promote-writers` (subagent, Cursor workers); then `verify-engage-from-idea`. The work is skill text with contract tests, so no frozen oracle is used.

Human stops: plan review (approved in the user's auto-execute request). Push is not a stop. Review runs once per phase; findings are fixed with tests and not re-reviewed unless the finding is major.

Collision notes:
- The two tasks touch disjoint files: `skills/engage/SKILL.md` and `tests/test_assess_routing.py`; `skills/init/SKILL.md`, `skills/settle-phase/SKILL.md` and their tests.
- The suite gate is advisory (set by the user in `.canon/config.json`).

## Exit criteria

- Every acceptance passes.
- The skill contract tests accept every `canon` invocation in the new `engage`, `init` and `settle-phase` text.
- `scripts/test.sh` passes.
