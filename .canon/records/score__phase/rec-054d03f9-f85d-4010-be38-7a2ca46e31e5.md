## Problem

The dashboard reads `composer:idea` only as a per-effort backlog. It does not read `composer:area`. An idea that has an area and no effort is invisible.

## Approach

Add an `areas.py` loader, a Pydantic model, and `GET /api/areas`. Add an Areas section on Home and an area detail screen with the scope text and the idea list. Show an idea in both the area view and the effort backlog (decision `idea-both-fields`). Show open and promoted ideas, with promoted collapsed (decision `area-idea-states`). Keep the area view read-only (decision `area-view-read-only`).

## Exit criteria

Requirement ids come from the phase specification.

- R1 to R5, R11: `GET /api/areas` returns every active area with its scope text and its open and promoted ideas, excludes dismissed and superseded ideas, carries area and effort on each idea, shows an idea with both fields in the area list and the effort backlog, and returns an empty list for a store without areas (pytest on seeded stores).
- R6 to R10: The Home screen has an Areas section with open idea counts; the area screen shows the scope text and the idea list (open first, promoted collapsed, linked to their effort or phase) with no promote or dismiss action; `#/area/<subject>` is a deep link that reloads to the same screen (Vitest).
- Integration: the web dashboard run against this repo's store lists the areas `dashboard`, `architecture`, and `agent-slop-standards` through `dashboard/bin/web` (test through the real entry point).
