## Problem

Phase 2 of the monorepo plan moves each component's tests to `tests/<component>/`. `tests/canon/` and `tests/tuner/` are there. Score, conductor, composer, stage, and installer tests are still files directly under `tests/`.

## Approach

Tests follow the import root (`tests-follow-import-root`). `orchestra.score` goes to `tests/score/`, `orchestra.conductor` to `tests/conductor/`, `orchestra.composer` to `tests/composer/`, `orchestra.stage` to `tests/stage/`, and `installer` to `tests/installer/`. Those directories use the root `tests/conftest.py` and are collected by the existing orchestra pytest process. Canon and tuner stay on their own processes. Dashboard, CLI, session, and other product tests stay directly under `tests/`, including every path the `wheel-contents` and `dashboard-split` specifications name.

## Exit criteria

- Score, conductor, composer, stage, and installer tests are collected from `tests/score/`, `tests/conductor/`, `tests/composer/`, `tests/stage/`, and `tests/installer/`.
- `tests/test_cli_packaging.py`, `tests/test_dashboard_model.py`, `tests/test_dashboard_app.py`, and `tests/test_web_dashboard.py` remain at those paths.
- `scripts/test.sh` does not ignore the new directories, and it exits 0.
- Every acceptance passes.

## Proof

kind: commands. A human runs `scripts/test.sh` and lists the new test directories.
