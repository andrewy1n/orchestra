## Problem

The composer pieces prove themselves only together: an idea is captured, triaged, shown on the roadmap and turned into an effort.

## Approach

Chosen: one test module drives the installed `orchestra` and `canon` entry points against a temporary store (`e2e-engage-coverage`). It runs `orchestra idea`, replays the documented triage, effort and promote `canon` calls, and checks `orchestra view roadmap` before and after promotion. The phase starts after `engage-from-idea` merges.

## Exit criteria

- `tests/test_composer_e2e.py` passes through the installed `orchestra` entry point.
- `canon validate` on the test store reports no errors.
- `scripts/test.sh` passes.

## Proof

kind: commands. A human reads the check-run for the e2e test.
