## Problem

The live store exists only as files. Moving it to a DB needs a lossless import, and a backup path that does not depend on git.

## Approach

Add `canon export` (deterministic JSONL of ops) and `canon import` (atomic, into an empty store). Prove the import with `scripts/verify_import.py`, which compares every folded revision and every history revision to the source files. Plan-phase decides the task split.

## Exit criteria

- R1 to R20 of the specification pass. Verified by the unit tests and `scripts/verify_import.py` against a copy of the real store.
- `scripts/verify_import.py` prints `0 mismatches`. Verified by running it.
- The round trip DB export, import, export gives identical files. Verified by a test.

## Proof

kind: commands. A human runs `scripts/verify_import.py` on a copy of the real store and sees `0 mismatches`.
