## Description

R13: `tests/test_boundaries.py` reports a module under `orchestra/composer/` that imports `orchestra.conductor`.
R14: `tests/test_boundaries.py` accepts a module under `orchestra/composer/` that imports `orchestra.canon` or `orchestra.score`.

## Approach

In `tests/test_boundaries.py` add `composer` to `COMPONENTS`, add `"composer": {"canon", "score", "composer"}` to `ALLOWED`, and add `composer` to the `stage` set. Add `orchestra/composer/schemas/composer.json` to `SCHEMA_FILES` in `tests/conftest.py` and `tests/tuner/conftest.py`, and to `SCHEMA_PATH` in `tests/canon_support.py`. Leave the `score,conductor,tuner` store lists unchanged. Do not edit files that have uncommitted work in the primary checkout.
