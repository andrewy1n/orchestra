## Description

R8: `init` supersedes the idea to `promoted` with the new effort after it creates the effort.
R9: `settle-phase` supersedes the idea to `promoted` with the effort and the new phase after it creates the phase.

## Approach

Edit `skills/init/SKILL.md`, `skills/settle-phase/SKILL.md` and their tests. In `init`, after the step that creates the new effort and its position, add: when the routing line carries `idea: <subject>`, read the idea (`canon list --type composer:idea --subject <idea-subject> --exact-subject --state active --full`) and supersede it with a fenced `canon supersede --type composer:idea --id <id> --expected-revision <revision> --payload '{"status":"promoted","effort":"<effort-slug>"}'`. In `settle-phase`, in step 1 after a `phase: new` phase is created, add the same read and a supersede with payload `{"status":"promoted","effort":"<effort-slug>","phase":"<phase-slug>"}`. Skip both when the idea is `none`. Tests (next to `tests/test_settle_phase_skill.py`; add `tests/test_init_idea_promote.py` for init): assert the text and that `extract_invocations` finds a `composer:idea` supersede in each skill whose payload carries `promoted`, `effort` and, for settle-phase, `phase`.
