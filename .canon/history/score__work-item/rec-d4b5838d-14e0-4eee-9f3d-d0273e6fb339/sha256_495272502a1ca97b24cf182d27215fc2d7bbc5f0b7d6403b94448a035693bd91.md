## Description

Skills, the dashboard, session scripts, agents, and the plugin manifests still live in this repo. They become the first commit of `~/orchestra`.

## Approach

1. Create `~/orchestra` as a new git repository. Do not push. Do not copy `.artifacts`.
2. Copy `skills/`, `dashboard/`, `scripts/`, `agents/`, `contract/`, `.cursor-plugin/`, `.claude-plugin/`, `pytest.ini`, `README.md`, `tests/conftest.py`, `tests/helpers.py`, and every test module except `test_affidavit_check.py`, `test_close_batch.py`, `test_verdict.py`, `test_manifest.py`, `test_amendment.py`, `test_oracle_red.py`, and `test_affidavit_aa.py`.
3. Copy `affidavit/reviewer.py` and `affidavit/suite.py` to `orchestra/reviewer.py` and `orchestra/suite.py`. Add `orchestra/__init__.py`. Delete the `tier2_*` aliases at the bottom of `suite.py`. Do not copy `affidavit/tier2.py`.
4. In the copied tree, replace imports of `affidavit.reviewer` and `affidavit.suite` with `orchestra.reviewer` and `orchestra.suite`. Leave imports of the evidence modules (`affidavit.check`, `affidavit.verdict`, and the rest) pointing at `affidavit`.
5. Set both plugin manifests' `name` to `orchestra`. Keep the skill slugs `engage`, `discuss`, `plan-phase`, `execute-phase`, `verify-work`, `init`, and `analyze-sessions`.
6. Add `tests/test_orchestra_package.py`: importing `orchestra.reviewer` and `orchestra.suite` succeeds; importing `orchestra.tier2` raises `ImportError`; `affidavit/tier2.py` is absent from this repo.
7. First commit subject `feat(orchestra): move the orchestration plugin out of affidavit`, with a one- or two-sentence body on why. Do not delete the originals from the affidavit checkout.
