## Problem

Work that is noticed but not yet part of a phase has nowhere to go in the store. It lives in chat or in GitHub issues outside the store. `project:feedback` only routes immediate new intent into discuss; it is not a parkable backlog.

## Approach

Add a `project:backlog-item` record type (effort-scoped), keep `feedback` unchanged, and document capture and promote through engage into discuss or incidental work. Detail settled in discuss decisions and design.

## Exit criteria

- The bundled contract resolves `project:backlog-item` and still resolves `project:feedback` with unchanged engage semantics.
- Skills document create, list, and promote for backlog items with CLI invocations the contract allows; `tests/test_skill_contract_consistency.py` passes.
- A seeded store can create, list open items for an effort, and promote or dismiss an item without treating it as feedback.
