## Problem

The dashboard reads `composer:idea` only as a per-effort backlog. It does not read `composer:area`. An idea that has an area and no effort is invisible.

## Approach

Add a backend loader for areas with their ideas, an API endpoint, an Areas section on the home screen, and an area detail screen with the scope text and the idea list. Reuse the backlog promote and dismiss actions. How to treat an idea that has both an effort and an area, and which idea states to show, are decided at settle-phase.

## Exit criteria

- With a seeded store of two areas and their ideas, the areas endpoint returns them (pytest).
- The Areas screen renders the seeded areas and ideas (Vitest).
- Every acceptance passes, and the web dashboard run against this repo's store lists the areas `dashboard`, `architecture`, and `agent-slop-standards` (integration check through the real entry point).
