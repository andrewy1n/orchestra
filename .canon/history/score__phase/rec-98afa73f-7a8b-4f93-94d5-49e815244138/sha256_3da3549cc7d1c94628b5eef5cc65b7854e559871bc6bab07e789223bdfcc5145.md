## Problem

The dashboard shows efforts in a flat rail. Areas and the product roadmap have no page above the efforts. The area page lists ideas only.

## Approach

Build on the `effort-area-link` phase. Three screens, all driven by the effort's placement:

- The area page at `#/area/<subject>` gains rollup stats, a list of its efforts with phase progress, an idea funnel, and one merged Needs-you count.
- A roadmap page at `#/roadmap` shows area lanes: one row per area, effort pills with phase progress, open ideas as dashed ghosts, and an Unassigned row.
- The effort page gains an area chip and a breadcrumb that link up to the area page.

Settled by the user from the spikes: area page (idea 3), lanes as the roadmap (idea 5), breadcrumb chip (idea 6). The board (idea 4) and the grouped rail (idea 1) are out. Detail is settled at `settle-phase` for this phase.

## Exit criteria

Decide at settle-phase. The phase still ends with every acceptance passing and the Playwright test loading the roadmap and an area page through the real server.
