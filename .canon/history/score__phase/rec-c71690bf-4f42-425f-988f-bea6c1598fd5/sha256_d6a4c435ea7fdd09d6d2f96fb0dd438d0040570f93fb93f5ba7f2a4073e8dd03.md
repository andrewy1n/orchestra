## Problem

Techniques (verdict, audit, probes) produce results, but humans and future agents have no stable, re-checkable trail that ties those results to claims. Without an evidence interface, "done" stays chat or suite-green.

## Approach

decide at plan-phase: observation ledger next to claims; verdict records; trail/show; bind made explicit. Pause polishing techniques until they speak Observation.

## Exit criteria

decide at plan-phase: a fixture proves verdict → recorded observation → trail shows it → recheck still works; schema and store location documented in the phase design.
