## Problem

Product planning has no home. Ideas live as `score:backlog-item` and `score:feedback`, which belong to work planning. The monorepo plan names `orchestra.composer` with `composer:idea` and `composer:area`.

## Approach

Add the `orchestra.composer` package and its `composer` schema module (version 1.0.0), registered in the `canon.schemas` entry points. `composer:area` is a product area (`composer-area-meaning`). `composer:idea` uses the `current` lifecycle with `status` open | promoted | dismissed (`composer-idea-lifecycle`) and optional refs to `composer:area`, `score:effort` and `score:phase`. composer sits above score: it imports only canon and score, and score does not change (`composer-score-direction`). Extend `tests/test_boundaries.py` and the test conftests. Add composer to the live store.

## Exit criteria

- `canon init --schemas score,conductor,tuner,composer` creates a store, and `canon create` writes an idea and an area (integration test against a temporary store).
- `tests/test_boundaries.py` passes with composer's import rules.
- The packaging test discovers `composer` from an installed prefix.
- `canon schemas add composer` on the live store, then `canon validate` reports no errors (manual check-run).

## Proof

kind: claims. A human reads the check-runs for the schema, boundary and packaging tests, and the live-store validate output.
