## Problem

Close matches an acceptance to a claim in `.affidavit/manifest.json` by id. It then requires `aff verdict` at the close revision and `aff recheck` on the live store. Those two reads can disagree, and a missing acceptance is skipped.

## Approach

**Both must pass** (`claim-close-gate`, `call-style`).

Close reads only `payload.claim`. It runs verdict at the green revision and recheck on the live binding, calling the affidavit functions. Either failure refuses, and the error names verdict, recheck, or both. A missing acceptance refuses. A claim id that is not in the manifest refuses. An acceptance with an empty `claim` is not put through this gate.

## Exit criteria

R1–R9 in the phase specification hold under the close-batch tests. A fixture where verdict fails and recheck passes refuses with an error that names verdict. A missing acceptance refuses.

## Proof

kind: claims

A human reads close-batch tests for verdict-only failure, recheck-only failure, both failing, a missing acceptance, and an unknown claim id.
