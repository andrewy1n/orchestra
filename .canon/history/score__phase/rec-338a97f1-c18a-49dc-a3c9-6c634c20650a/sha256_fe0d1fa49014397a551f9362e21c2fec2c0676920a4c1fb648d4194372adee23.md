## Problem

The evidence layer and the orchestration loop (skills, dashboard, session scripts) share one repo and one contract, so nothing stops a skill from writing evidence directly.

## Approach

Verbs stay in this repo; the orchestration surfaces move to `~/orchestra` (decisions: `repo-split`, `verb-home`, `orchestra-name`, `contract-project`, `effort-records`). Spec/design: `orchestration-split`.

This repo keeps `aff check`, `aff close-batch`, and `aff verdict`. Reviewer, suite, tier2, the skills, the dashboard, the session scripts, and the plugin manifests move. The repo and plugin are named orchestra. Skill slugs stay. The bundled contract's `"project"` becomes `orchestra`, and ensure-store rewrites an existing `affidavit` project field to that. Records for `dashboard`, `workflow-loop`, `packaging`, `session-analysis`, and `test-suite-speed` move to the orchestra store. This store keeps `evidence-layer`.

Skills call `aff check` for `tdd` and `check` results and `aff verdict` for a claim in the manifest. They do not create those check-runs or a `project:oracle-amendment`.

## Exit criteria

This repo's tests pass with `skills/`, `dashboard/`, `scripts/`, and `agents/` absent, and `aff check`, `aff close-batch`, and `aff verdict` still run. A test in the orchestra repo fails when a skill creates a `tdd` or `check` check-run or a `project:oracle-amendment`. Both stores validate: `evidence-layer` is the live goal here, and the five moved efforts are the live goals in orchestra.
