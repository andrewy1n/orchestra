## Problem

The dashboard shows efforts in a flat rail. Areas have no page above the efforts, and the area page lists ideas only. A reader cannot move from the product down to an effort, or back up.

## Approach

Build the Product and Area levels of the drill-down, and give the effort its own URL. Settled by the user from the spikes. Decisions: `landing-product-index`, `rail-siblings`, `area-needs-read-only`, `area-join-client-side`.

- `#/` becomes the product index: one card for each area, the whole card is the link, no button. Efforts with no area show in a "Not in an area" group.
- `#/area/<subject>` shows scope, rollup stats, a merged read-only Needs-you list, the Efforts rows, an Ideas section, and "Started as efforts" lines for promoted ideas.
- `#/effort/<name>` shows the existing effort Home with an area chip. A breadcrumb replaces the top-bar crumbs on every screen.
- The left rail lists the siblings of the current level.
- Group efforts in the browser from the snapshot. The backend adds promoted ideas to the area where their effort is placed.

The phase overlay stays. Phase `phase-level` turns it into a routed page. Rejected after the spikes: a roadmap board, a lanes page, tabs, an accordion, a sidebar list of areas, a grouped effort rail, inline expandable phases.

Plan: seven tasks in five waves, then the closing verification task.

1. `areas-promoted-ideas` (backend, R14) and `area-state` (route, pure functions, R18) run together.
2. `breadcrumb-rail` adds the Breadcrumb and the level-aware Rail.
3. `product-index` adds the product index at `#/` and moves the old tests that mount an effort at `#/` to `#/effort/<name>`.
4. `area-page` rewrites the area page, and `e2e-area-seed` seeds an area in the Playwright store. They run together.
5. `area-chip-started` adds the area chip and the "Started as efforts" lines.

Frontend acceptances are thin `tdd` checks, one per requirement. The test name starts with the requirement id. The backend acceptance for R14 is oracle-backed: an oracle author writes the test from the requirement text, and the parent freezes it after an assertion-level red.
