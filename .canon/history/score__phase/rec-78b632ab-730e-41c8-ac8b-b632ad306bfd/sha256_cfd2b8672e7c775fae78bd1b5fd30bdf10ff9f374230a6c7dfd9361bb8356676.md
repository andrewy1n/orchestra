## Problem

The dashboard shows efforts in a flat rail. Areas have no page above the efforts, and the area page lists ideas only. A reader cannot move from the product down to an effort, or back up.

## Approach

Build on `effort-area-link`. Settled from the spikes (unified drill-down, levels Product › Area › Effort › Phase):

- A breadcrumb on every screen. Each part but the last is a link.
- A product index at `#/areas`: one card per area with the title, the first line of its scope, counts and its effort names. The whole card is the link. There is no button. A group for efforts with no area follows the cards.
- The area page at `#/area/<subject>`: scope text, rollup stats, a merged Needs-you block, an Efforts section of rows that are whole-row links, and a separate Ideas section. Ideas are plain notes with no progress and no funnel. A "Started as efforts" list under Ideas shows promoted ideas as reference lines that link to their efforts.
- The effort screen gains an area chip that links to the area page, and the breadcrumb.
- The left rail lists the siblings of the current level: areas at the area level, and the efforts of the same area at the effort level.

The phase stays an overlay in this phase. Phase `phase-level` turns it into a routed page.

Rejected after the spikes: a roadmap board, a lanes page, tabs, an accordion, a sidebar list, a grouped effort rail, inline expandable phases.

## Exit criteria

Decide at settle-phase. The phase still ends with every acceptance passing and the Playwright test loading the product index and an area page through the real server.
