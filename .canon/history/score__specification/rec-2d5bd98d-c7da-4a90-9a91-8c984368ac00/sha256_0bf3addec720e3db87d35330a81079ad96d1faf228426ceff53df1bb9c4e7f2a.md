## Requirements

R1: `#/` shows the product index with one card for each area.
R2: An area card shows the area title.
R3: An area card shows the first sentence of the area's scope.
R4: An area card shows the number of efforts in the area.
R5: An area card shows the number of open ideas in the area.
R6: An area card lists the names of the efforts in the area.
R7: An area card is one link to `#/area/<subject>` and contains no button.
R8: Efforts with no area show under a "Not in an area" heading on the product index.
R9: The product index has no left rail.
R10: The area page lists each effort of the area as a row that links to `#/effort/<name>`.
R11: The area page shows the open ideas of the area in an Ideas section.
R12: The area page shows promoted ideas whose effort is placed in the area under a "Started as efforts" heading.
R13: Each line under "Started as efforts" links to its effort.
R14: `GET /api/areas` lists, for each area, the promoted ideas whose effort is placed in that area.
R15: The area page shows one Needs-you list that merges the items of the efforts in the area.
R16: Each Needs-you row on the area page names its effort and links to that effort's page.
R17: The area page has no Needs-you action buttons.
R18: `#/effort/<name>` shows the Home screen of that effort.
R19: The effort page shows an area chip that links to `#/area/<subject>` when the effort has an area.
R20: The breadcrumb on the area page reads `Product › <area title>`.
R21: The breadcrumb on the page of an effort with an area reads `Product › <area title> › <effort>`.
R22: The breadcrumb on the page of an effort with no area reads `Product › <effort>`.
R23: Every breadcrumb part except the last is a link.
R24: On the area page the left rail lists the areas.
R25: On the effort page the left rail lists the efforts of the same area, or the efforts with no area when the effort has none.
R26: The show-finished toggle still hides and shows finished efforts in the effort rail.
R27: The existing `#/phase/...`, `#/task/...` and `#/journal/...` deep links open as before.
R28: The Playwright test loads `#/` and sees a seeded area card.
R29: The Playwright test clicks that card and sees the area page.

## Acceptance criteria

- R1 to R9, R19 to R26: unit tests in `dashboard/frontend/src` (vitest, jsdom) render the screens against a fixture snapshot and areas response.
- R10 to R13, R15 to R17: unit tests render the area page against the same fixtures.
- R14: a backend test in `tests/dashboard` seeds a promoted idea and a placement and reads `GET /api/areas`.
- R18: a unit test mounts the app at `#/effort/<name>`.
- R27: the existing deep-link and overlay tests pass unchanged.
- R28, R29: the Playwright test in `dashboard/frontend/e2e` passes locally and in CI.

## Constraints

- Phase `effort-area-link` is merged into `main` before this phase starts.
- The product index and the area page write nothing to the store.
- Routing stays hash based. The server still serves one document.
- No new dependencies. Reuse the existing shadcn-svelte components.
- `npm run lint`, `npm run check` and `npm test` pass. `basedpyright` and `ruff` pass.
- `src/api-types.ts` stays in sync with `openapi.json`.

## Non-goals

- The phase as a routed page. Phase `phase-level` does that. The phase overlay stays as it is.
- A roadmap board, lanes page, tabs, accordion or sidebar list of areas.
- Any UI to create an area or to move an effort between areas.
- Changing how ideas are captured, triaged or promoted.
- Needs-you answers or sign-offs on the area page.

## Invariants

- The URL is the source of truth for the selected effort.
- Every screen above the effort level is read-only.
- An idea never appears among the efforts of an area.
- An effort belongs to at most one area.

## Assumptions

- Each effort in the snapshot carries `area`, as phase `effort-area-link` adds.
- `GET /api/areas` already returns the open ideas of each area.
- The existing Home screen and phase overlay need no change to open from `#/effort/<name>`.
