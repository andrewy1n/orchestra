## Requirements

R1: A test creates a temporary store with `canon init --schemas score,conductor,tuner,composer`.
R2: The test runs `orchestra idea` through the installed entry point and creates an open idea.
R3: The test creates an area and assigns it to the idea with the `canon` calls `triage-ideas` documents.
R4: The test runs `orchestra view roadmap` through the installed entry point and finds the idea under its area.
R5: The test creates the effort with the `canon` calls `init` documents.
R6: The test promotes the idea with the `canon` call `init` documents for the engage path.
R7: The test runs `orchestra view roadmap` again and finds the idea as promoted with its effort.
R8: `canon validate` on the temporary store reports no errors at the end.
R9: The test never reads or writes the live store.
R10: `scripts/test.sh` passes.

## Acceptance criteria

- R1 to R9: one test module, `tests/test_composer_e2e.py`, passes against the installed prefix.
- R10: the full suite passes.

## Constraints

- The test calls `orchestra` and `canon` as subprocesses from the installed prefix, as `tests/test_cli_packaging.py` does.
- The `canon` calls are written out in the test. They match the invocations the skills document; the skill contract tests keep the skill text valid.
- The test is the only new file besides fixtures.

## Non-goals

- Running a model or a skill.
- Testing the dashboard.
- Covering failure paths. The earlier phases' unit tests do.

## Invariants

- The test leaves no state outside its temporary directory.

## Assumptions

- Phases `backlog-to-idea`, `idea-triage` and `engage-from-idea` merge first.
- The `installed` fixture in `tests/test_cli_packaging.py` can serve the new module, or moves to `tests/conftest.py`.
