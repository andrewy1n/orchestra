## Rationale

One data path means the status line and the app cannot disagree. Measured: load_snapshot takes about 0.25 s, the same as the current status line, well inside the 2 s timeout, and dashboard_model runs under plain python3.

## Counter-argument

The snapshot makes about nine CLI calls where the view makes one; on a much larger store it could near the 2 s timeout, and the status line then prints nothing.
