## Rationale

Requirements as a clickable strip reuses evidence already on phase detail and gives a path into requirement overlays without opening the Evidence tab first.

## Counter-argument

Leaving requirements only on the Evidence tab avoids duplicate UI. That was rejected because overview should answer "is this phase healthy?" without a tab switch.
